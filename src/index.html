About

I'm a final-year data science student. Most of what I enjoy sits before the model: finding out how a dataset was actually collected, what the missing rows mean, and whether the thing being measured is the thing anyone cares about.

Last summer I worked as a research assistant on the reef imagery project, which meant a lot of time defending label decisions to marine biologists. It made me much more careful about what a metric is really claiming.

Outside coursework I maintain the lecture-notes search tool for my cohort and help run the university's data society workshops on reproducible analysis.
